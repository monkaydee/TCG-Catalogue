# Features competitors have that CardNavo lacks (8 October 2026)

Extends [COMPETITOR-COMPARISON-2026-10-08.md](COMPETITOR-COMPARISON-2026-10-08.md); gaps listed there (trade calculator,
advanced filters/folders, Pokédex view, friends/Trade Finder, web/iOS, licensed sold history) are not repeated.
Sources are official pages, store listings and help docs; competitor statements are their claims, not tested.
"Unverified" = the page could not be loaded and only a search snippet was seen.

**Already equal or better:** centering tool, free widgets, stack scan, price alerts (singles and sealed), free CSV/PDF export,
exact language/printing/grade valuations with honest unknowns, cost ledger/PnL/grading tracker/insurance export,
several binders with covers, native JP printings and 21 UI languages, sealed inventory, sync without account.
Competitors charge (Collectr Pro $4.99–7.99/mo, Dex+ $3.99/mo, Shiny Pro $4.99/mo, Ludex $4.99–24.99/mo) for
scanning, export or widgets that CardNavo gives free.

**Recommended next:** 1) "Worth grading?" 2) cost to complete a set 3) CSV import from other apps
4) slab cert barcode scan 5) more display currencies + bulk multi-select.

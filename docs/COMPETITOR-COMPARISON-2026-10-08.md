# Collection-app comparison — 8 October 2026

Reviewed official product pages and compared against this repository. These are advertised
capabilities, not hands-on verification of competitor accuracy or every subscription tier.
No competitor code/assets or user uploads were copied. Shopping remains a proposal.

| Capability advertised elsewhere | Source | CardNavo now | Gap / realistic next step |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Trade Analyzer: two sides, singles/sealed/cash, condition/grade and manual prices | [Collectr Trade](https://getcollectr.com/trade) | Trade list flags and export; no two-sided valuation screen found | Add a local trade calculator after quote reliability; unknown prices must remain unknown |
| In-app sold comparables, broader graded data, up to 5+ years of history, marketplace/watchlist | [Collectr Prices](https://getcollectr.com/prices) | Own recorded history; exact-grade quotes/provider-backed PSA population; external sold links | Licensed sold-history access and coverage are missing; UI cannot create provider rights/data |
| Friends, collection comparison and missing-card Trade Finder | [Dex](https://dextcg.com/) | No accounts, friend graph or hosted public collections | Optional future service; requires authentication/privacy/moderation operations |
| Pokémon species Pokédex and custom folders; international/JP/Simplified Chinese expansion coverage | [Dex](https://dextcg.com/) | Set checklist, wishlist and game/set binder; regional catalogue coverage incomplete | Local species/custom collection views are feasible; regional data must be curated |
| Detailed regional variants, condition/language/rarity filters and custom lists | [TCG Collector](https://www.tcgcollector.com/) | Language/condition/printing at entry; lists for wishlist/trade/sold; collection filters mostly game/set/sort | Add advanced collection filters and named custom lists; verify obscure master-set printings |
| iPhone/Android/browser access | [Dex](https://dextcg.com/) | Android only; chosen-file cloud backup/sync | iOS/web clients would be separate engineering projects; no account-based live sync exists |
| More tracked games and multiple price integrations | [Collectr](https://getcollectr.com/), [Dex](https://dextcg.com/) | Eight game IDs; EUR/USD display; provider support differs by game/language | Expand only after regional price/image coverage diagnostics, not just more game buttons |

Already present in CardNavo: local OCR/photo/stack recognition, raw and company-specific
slabs, virtual curved binder, centering pre-grade reveal/CSV/saved reports, sealed inventory,
condition/language/printing selection, cost/submission ledger, PnL/sales, wishlist/alerts,
set checklists, exports, chosen-file backup/sync, 21 UI locales, themes and three configurable
widgets. Do not put these back on the roadmap as if absent.

## Prioritised ideas

1. Reliable exact-language/grade prices and an auditable coverage dashboard; approved sold data.
2. Private, rights-cleared recognition benchmark and meaningful accuracy gates.
3. Advanced filters: language, condition, printing, grader/grade, missing/stale/manual price,
   duplicates, trade status and custom folders. Keep portfolio unknowns visible.
4. Local trade calculator, with shipping/fees/cash and explicit unknown valuations.
5. Pokémon species collecting, native JP repair shortcuts and master-set variant progress.
6. Price alerts with evidence/freshness controls and deeper licensed history.
7. Go Shopping country pilot once feeds/access are secured; see existing plan. No implementation.
8. Account/social/web/iOS only with a separate product, security and operations decision.

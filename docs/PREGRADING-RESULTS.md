### Reading the table

- Knowing only the card's language and print year gives MAE 0.554. The learned picture features alone give 0.520; catalogue and pictures together 0.508.
- Compared card by card on the same test cards, adding the pictures to the catalogue facts lowers the error by **0.046 grades** (95 % interval +0.008 to +0.085). That is a real but small gain. Going from no information to the catalogue alone lowers it by 0.203. So most of what a picture model appears to know is which kinds of cards usually get which grade, not the condition of this copy.
- The app's own 50 measurements (centering and rim wear), fitted on these scans, score 0.613: worse than the catalogue alone.
- A model is only useful for pre-grading to the extent that it beats the catalogue-only row.

## Training configuration

- Feature network: MobileNetV3-small, ImageNet weights (torchvision, BSD), frozen. 28 close-ups of 256×256 scan pixels per card (4 corners and 10 edge windows per side) plus the whole card per side, each resized to 224×224; 720 numbers per view.
- Head: one logistic model per threshold ("grade k or better", k = 10 … 5) on standardised features; the chance of a lower bar is never below that of a higher one.
- Chosen on validation: learned C=0.01, PCA 256 components; measured C=0.01; combined C=0.03.
- Augmentation: none. Seed 7. Hardware: 2 CPU cores, no GPU.
- Fitting and scoring time: 40 s (after feature extraction, about 0.8 s per card).
